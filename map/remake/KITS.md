# Bộ kiến trúc modular v3

Bốn file `library_kit_v3.png`, `vault_kit_v3.png`, `furnace_kit_v3.png`, `tower_kit_v3.png` được tạo bằng image_gen tích hợp, dùng ảnh kiến trúc v2 của từng khu làm tham chiếu.

Mỗi ảnh gồm lưới 4×4: hàng đầu là vật liệu sàn; hàng hai là tường và hành lang; hai hàng cuối là 8 đạo cụ. Builder trích 8 tile 64×64, giữ collision ở hai tile tường; các đạo cụ chỉ vẽ trong ô tường. Mỗi khu nhỏ có tên, bố cục lát sàn và cách bố trí đạo cụ riêng. Tài nguyên này được dùng trong game chính.

## Prompt

### library

Production asset sheet for a top-down 2D RPG, matching the provided reference's hand-painted fantasy game-art materials. Generate an EXACT uniform 4-column by 4-row grid, square image 2048x2048. Every cell exactly 512x512; no margins, no gutters, no labels, no lines between cells. All elements obey orthographic top-down RPG camera with slight visible north-facing wall fronts, no perspective floor horizon. Theme: ancient grand library: warm cream sandstone octagonal paving, walnut parquet, burgundy carpet, aged brass; walls of walnut bookcases and sandstone piers; props: tightly packed bookcase, scroll cabinet, reading desk with books, brass globe, manuscript rack, candle stand, carved scholarly bust, archive chest. Precise cell contents in reading order: row 1: four different seamless flat walkable FLOOR material tiles, closely matching theme palette, no furniture or obstacles, fine texture, no perspective; row 2: cell1 straight horizontal WALL segment filling tile (solid architecture), cell2 alternate straight horizontal WALL segment filling tile, cell3 seamless walkable CORRIDOR FLOOR tile with restrained metal edging along left/right borders, cell4 walkable FLOOR tile with subtle inset decorative pattern; row3 and row4: eight unique separate architectural props listed in theme, one centered complete prop per cell, occupying about 80 percent of cell, shown on fully transparent background. Upper eight cells are fully opaque tile artwork filling each whole cell. Lower eight cells have true transparent background around the props. Props should be individually usable game assets, no cropped pieces, cast small soft contact shadows, consistent pixel density and light from upper left. No characters, writing, UI, borders or watermark. This is a modular asset atlas, NOT a full room scene. All sixteen cells MUST be square and equally sized. Distinct attractive detailed architecture.
